To qualify for a UK Skilled Worker visa in 2026 you must:

- Have a job offer from a licensed UK sponsor
- Have a certificate of sponsorship for the role
- Work in an eligible occupation
- Be paid at least £41,700 a year, or the going rate — whichever is higher
- Meet the English language requirement (B1 level)

Some jobs have a lower threshold of £33,400. Health and education roles follow national pay scales.

The Skilled Worker visa is the main UK work route to [settlement](/articles/life-in-the-uk-test-skilled-worker-ilr-requirements). To get it, you need a sponsored job that meets a salary threshold and an eligible occupation. This guide lists every requirement, the exact salary rules for 2026, and the exceptions that lower the bar. This covers the first application — the rules for [renewing a Skilled Worker visa](/articles/skilled-worker-visa-renewal-uk) are slightly different.

## What are the Skilled Worker visa requirements in 2026?

You must meet all of these:

1. A job offer from a UK employer with a sponsor licence
2. A certificate of sponsorship (CoS) from that employer
3. A job on the list of eligible occupations
4. A salary that meets the threshold and the going rate
5. English at B1 level
6. Enough personal savings, unless your sponsor certifies it

If any one is missing, the application fails. The salary rule causes the most refusals, so check it before you accept a job.

## The Skilled Worker salary threshold explained

You must be paid the **higher** of two figures:

- The general threshold of **£41,700 a year**, or
- The "going rate" for your specific occupation

Each eligible job has its own going rate published by the Home Office. If your job's going rate is £45,000, you must be paid at least £45,000 — the £41,700 floor is not enough on its own.

Some applicants qualify at a lower threshold of **£33,400 a year**. This can apply to new entrants to the labour market and certain other cases. Even at the lower threshold, you must still meet the going rate rules for your job.

## Which jobs use different salary rules

Not every role uses the standard going rate. Two groups follow different rules:

- **Health and care workers** — paid under NHS and national pay scales
- **Education roles** — teachers and similar roles on national pay scales

For these jobs, the national pay scale sets the salary, not the standard going rate table. If you work in [health or care](/articles/health-and-care-worker-visa-ilr), check the pay scale for your band rather than the £41,700 figure.

## The sponsor and certificate of sponsorship

You cannot apply for a Skilled Worker visa on your own. You need a UK employer that holds a **sponsor licence**. That employer gives you a **certificate of sponsorship** — an electronic record with a reference number for your job.

The certificate is not a paper document. It confirms your job title, salary, and start date. You enter its reference number when you apply. If your employer loses its licence, your visa can be affected — read what happens if a [sponsor licence is revoked](/articles/sponsor-licence-revoked-skilled-worker).

## English and savings requirements

You must prove English at **B1 level** — you can speak, read, write, and understand it. You show this with an approved test or a degree taught in English. This is the same level you will need again for [settlement and the Life in the UK stage](/articles/skilled-worker-visa-life-in-the-uk-test).

You also need enough personal savings to support yourself — usually **£1,270** held for 28 days before you apply. You do not need this if your sponsor ticks the box to certify your maintenance on the certificate of sponsorship.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Meeting £41,700 but missing the going rate**  
People see the £41,700 figure and assume any salary above it works. If your job's going rate is higher, you must be paid that. A salary below the going rate fails the application.

❌ **Assuming you can self-sponsor**  
You cannot sponsor yourself or use a friend's company informally. The employer must hold a genuine sponsor licence. Without one, there is no valid route.

❌ **Using an old salary threshold**  
The standard threshold rose to £41,700. Planning around older figures like £26,200 or £38,700 will leave you short and lead to refusal.

❌ **Forgetting the 28-day savings rule**  
If your sponsor does not certify maintenance, you need £1,270 held for 28 continuous days. Moving money in just before you apply does not count.

## Expert Tips (What Most Guides Miss)

1. **Check the going rate before you accept the offer.** The going rate is job-specific and often higher than £41,700. Confirm your salary clears it before you sign, not after.
2. **Ask if your sponsor will certify maintenance.** If they do, you skip the £1,270 savings rule entirely. It is a simple box on your certificate of sponsorship.
3. **Confirm your occupation is still eligible.** The eligible occupation list changes. A role that qualified last year may not now. Check the current list against your job code.
4. **Line up your English evidence early.** A degree taught in English can meet the requirement without a test. Get your official confirmation before you apply to avoid delays.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the minimum salary for a Skilled Worker visa in 2026?

The standard minimum is £41,700 a year, or the going rate for your job if that is higher. Some applicants qualify at a lower threshold of £33,400. You must always meet whichever figure is highest for your role.

### Can I get a Skilled Worker visa without a job offer?

No. You need a confirmed job offer from a UK employer that holds a sponsor licence, plus a certificate of sponsorship. You cannot self-sponsor or apply without an employer.

### What English level do I need for a Skilled Worker visa?

You need B1 level English — you can speak, read, write, and understand it. You prove this with an approved English test or a degree that was taught in English.

### Do health and care workers have a different salary threshold?

Yes. Health and care roles and education roles are paid under national pay scales rather than the standard going rate. Check the relevant pay scale for your band instead of the £41,700 figure.

### How much savings do I need for a Skilled Worker visa?

You usually need £1,270 held in your account for 28 days before you apply. You do not need this if your sponsor certifies your maintenance on the certificate of sponsorship.

### Does the Skilled Worker visa lead to settlement?

Yes. After 5 years on the Skilled Worker route, you can usually apply for ILR, provided you still meet the salary and other rules and pass the Life in the UK test.
