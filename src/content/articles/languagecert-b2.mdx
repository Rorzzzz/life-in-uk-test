## Who Is LanguageCert?

LanguageCert is a UK-based Ofqual-regulated awarding body. Its International ESOL exams are recognised by UKVI as Secure English Language Tests. It offers SELT exams at multiple CEFR levels, including B1 and B2.

For UK immigration, only the **UKVI SELT** versions of LanguageCert exams count. General LanguageCert certificates from non-UKVI sittings do not count for settlement.

## How to Prepare for LanguageCert B2

**1. Diagnose your level.**
Take a free B2 mock. Score each skill separately. Note the weakest area.

**2. Build a 10–12 week study plan.**
Aim for 4–6 hours per week split across:
- Reading practice (news articles, opinion pieces)
- Writing practice (2–3 short pieces per week)
- Listening (podcasts, recorded interviews)
- Speaking (recorded answers, conversation practice)

**3. Use official sample materials.**
LanguageCert publishes sample papers. Practise under timed conditions.

**4. Focus on writing.**
Most B2 failures are in writing. Get feedback from a teacher or a strong English speaker.

**5. Book with 4–6 weeks to go.**
Book far enough ahead to secure a slot near you, close enough to keep your prep momentum.

## LanguageCert B2 vs LanguageCert B1

| Feature | LanguageCert B1 | LanguageCert B2 |
|---|---|---|
| Skills tested | Speaking + Listening | Speaking, Listening, Reading, Writing |
| Length | Shorter | Longer (2–3 hours total) |
| Difficulty | Everyday topics | More abstract topics |
| Used for | ILR before 26 March 2027 | ILR from 26 March 2027 |
