B2 is one full CEFR level above B1. It tests all four skills — speaking, listening, reading and writing. B1 only tests speaking and listening. For most people, moving from B1 to B2 takes an extra 2–3 months of focused study. From 26 March 2027, B2 is the new minimum for UK settlement.

## The Big Difference — Two Skills vs Four Skills

**B1 (current rule until 26 March 2027)**
- Speaking (with an examiner)
- Listening (short recordings and prompts)
- No reading exam
- No writing exam
- Test lasts 15–25 minutes

**B2 (new rule from 26 March 2027)**
- Speaking (longer and more abstract)
- Listening (longer recordings, complex ideas)
- Reading (news-level texts, opinion pieces)
- Writing (essays, letters, structured responses)
- Test lasts 2–3 hours

The writing paper is what surprises most B1 passers. You cannot rely on natural conversation skills alone.

---

## CEFR — What B1 and B2 Actually Mean

**B1 (intermediate)**
You can handle most everyday situations while travelling. You can describe experiences, plans and give reasons for opinions.

**B2 (upper-intermediate)**
You can understand the main ideas of complex text. You can interact with fluent speakers without strain on either side. You can produce clear, detailed text on a wide range of subjects.

The gap sounds small on paper. In practice, B2 needs a much bigger vocabulary and better grammar control.

## Study Time — Realistic Expectations

Every learner is different. As a guide:

- **B1 from scratch:** 3–6 months of regular study
- **B1 → B2:** 2–3 months of focused study
- **B2 from scratch:** 6–12 months of regular study

If you already hold B1 and use English at work every day, 6–8 weeks of writing-focused prep may be enough. If you rarely write in English, plan for longer.

## The IELTS Score Rule at B2

For B2 with IELTS for UKVI, you must score:

- **Listening:** 5.5 or higher
- **Reading:** 5.5 or higher
- **Writing:** 5.5 or higher
- **Speaking:** 5.5 or higher

Any component below 5.5 fails the whole requirement. This is a common trap — people focus on their overall band and miss that one section brought them under.

At B1 with IELTS Life Skills you only had to pass listening and speaking. Full context: [IELTS Life Skills B2 explainer](/articles/ielts-life-skills-b2).

## Common Mistakes When Moving From B1 to B2

- ❌ Booking too soon — the writing skill takes longest to build
- ❌ Assuming B2 speaking is just longer B1 speaking (it needs more abstract vocabulary)
- ❌ Booking a **standard IELTS** instead of **IELTS for UKVI**
- ❌ Searching for "IELTS Life Skills B2" — not a real product
- ❌ Ignoring the reading paper — timing is tight
- ❌ Waiting until March 2027 — SELT centres get busy

## Expert Tips to Jump From B1 to B2

- 📝 Read 1 opinion article a day in English — BBC or The Guardian work well
- 📝 Write 1 short essay (200–250 words) three times a week
- 📝 Record yourself answering B2 speaking prompts
- 📝 Practise listening at 1.25x speed to train comprehension
- 📝 Learn 500 mid-frequency academic words in 8 weeks
- 📝 Do 2 full mock tests before booking

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is B2 really much harder than B1?**
Yes. Writing is the biggest step up. Reading time pressure also surprises people.

**Can my B1 certificate count for B2?**
No. Each SELT is level-specific. You need a B2 pass to meet the new rule.

**Do I have to take B2 in 2026?**
Not yet. Until **26 March 2027**, B1 still applies for most routes.

**Can I still use IELTS Life Skills at B2?**
No. Life Skills goes only up to B1. See [IELTS Life Skills B2](/articles/ielts-life-skills-b2).

**Which is cheapest at B2?**
It depends on your area. Compare all four providers — [B2 English test UK hub](/articles/b2-english-test).

**Does the Life in the UK test go up in difficulty too?**
No. The Life in the UK test is separate and unchanged. Practise with [60 free mock tests](/mock-test).
