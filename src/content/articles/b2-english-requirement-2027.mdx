From **26 March 2027**, most UK settlement (ILR) applications require **B2 English** — one step up from the current B1 rule. This is confirmed law under Statement of Changes HC 1691, published on 6 March 2026. If you apply before 26 March 2027, B1 still applies.

## Confirmed Law vs Proposal — What This Actually Is

This section matters. There is a lot of confused reporting online.

**What is confirmed:**
- Statement of Changes HC 1691 was laid before Parliament and published on **6 March 2026**
- It changes the English language rule for settlement from **B1 to B2**
- The change takes effect on **26 March 2027**
- This is legislation — not a consultation or proposal

**What is still developing:**
- Transitional rules for people who already hold B1 evidence
- Exact evidence lists for exemption categories
- Guidance for citizenship applications that follow B1-route ILR

Always check GOV.UK closer to the date. Rules can be updated by later statements.

## Who Is Affected

Under HC 1691, the following main routes now need B2 for settlement from 26 March 2027:

- **Family route** — spouse, partner, unmarried partner, parent
- **Skilled Worker**
- **High Potential Individual (HPI)**
- **Scale-up**

If you are on one of these routes, plan for a B2 test unless you are exempt.

**Likely exemptions** (based on the current B1 framework — subject to confirmation):
- Nationals of majority English-speaking countries
- Applicants with a degree taught in English (evidence rules apply)
- Applicants aged 65 or over
- Applicants with a qualifying long-term physical or mental condition

For the current B1 exemption list, see [Do I need a B1 English test for ILR?](/articles/do-i-need-a-b1-english-test-for-ilr).

## What Counts as a B2 Pass

A B2 pass must come from an approved Secure English Language Test (SELT) provider:

- **Trinity College London** — ISE II or higher (B2)
- **IELTS SELT Consortium** — IELTS for UKVI Academic or General Training, 5.5 in all four components
- **LanguageCert** — Academic, General, or International ESOL SELT at B2
- **Pearson** — PTE Academic UKVI or PTE Home at B2

There is no "IELTS Life Skills B2". Life Skills tops out at B1. See our full explainer: [IELTS Life Skills B2](/articles/ielts-life-skills-b2).

## Why the Government Raised the Bar

Home Office explanatory notes with HC 1691 point to two reasons:

- Better English improves integration and employment outcomes
- The B2 standard aligns UK settlement with several comparable countries

The Home Office confirmed that citizenship route language rules are being kept under review separately. B2 for citizenship has not yet been legislated.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**When exactly does B2 English start?**
26 March 2027. Confirmed by HC 1691 on 6 March 2026.

**Is this really law or just a plan?**
It is law. It was laid before Parliament as a Statement of Changes to the Immigration Rules.

**Does the change apply to citizenship too?**
Not yet in the same Statement of Changes. Citizenship rules on English are kept under review.

**Do I still need the Life in the UK test?**
Yes. Life in the UK and B2 English are separate requirements. See [Is the Life in the UK test an English test?](/articles/is-the-life-in-the-uk-test-an-english-test).

**Can I use my existing B1 certificate after 26 March 2027?**
Likely not for ILR under B2 rules. Transitional guidance is still developing — check GOV.UK.

**What happens if I send B1 evidence by mistake?**
GOV.UK is clear: **"Your citizenship or settlement application will be refused if you send the wrong qualifications."**
