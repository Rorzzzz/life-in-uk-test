## What Is the B1 English Requirement?

B1 is the third level on the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR). At B1, you can:

- Understand the main points of clear, standard speech on familiar topics
- Deal with most situations likely to arise when travelling in an English-speaking area
- Produce simple, connected text on familiar topics
- Describe experiences, events, and briefly give reasons for opinions

For immigration purposes, the Home Office requires B1 specifically in **speaking and listening** — not writing.

## Who Needs a B1 English Test?

You need to prove B1 English if you are applying for:

- **Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR)** — most visa routes require B1 at the ILR stage
- **British citizenship by naturalisation** — B1 is the requirement and is not changing
- **Some family visas** — partners and spouses on the five-year route require B1 at settlement

**Important 2026 update:** From 8 January 2026, new Skilled Worker visa applications require English at **B2 level**. However, ILR applicants who entered on an existing Skilled Worker visa still only need **B1 until 26 March 2027**, when ILR will also rise to B2.

British citizenship remains B1 and this is not currently changing.

## Who Is Exempt?

You do not need to take a B1 test if you:

- Are a national of a majority English-speaking country (UK, USA, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Ireland and others on the approved list)
- Have a degree that was taught and assessed in English — from a UK degree or a qualification from an approved country
- Are aged 65 or over
- Have a physical or mental condition that prevents you from meeting the requirement

If you have already passed a B1 test for a previous visa application (e.g., your initial Skilled Worker visa), you may be able to use that result again for ILR — check the current GOV.UK guidance for your route.

## Which B1 Tests Are Approved?

The Home Office only accepts results from Secure English Language Tests (SELTs). The main approved tests are:

| Test | Provider | Format | Cost |
|------|----------|--------|------|
| IELTS Life Skills B1 | British Council / IDP | Face-to-face | ~£150–175 |
| Trinity GESE Grade 5 | Trinity College London | Face-to-face | ~£120–150 |
| LanguageCert International ESOL SELT B1 | LanguageCert | Online or in-person | ~£100–140 |
| PSI SELT B1 | PSI Services | Online | ~£130–160 |
| Pearson PTE Academic UKVI | Pearson | Online | ~£150–175 |

Prices vary by test centre and location. Always book through the official provider website and check that your test centre is UKVI-approved.

**Note:** General IELTS Academic, IELTS General Training (non-UKVI), and other mainstream English certificates are **not** accepted for immigration purposes.

## What the B1 Speaking and Listening Test Involves

Most approved B1 tests (especially IELTS Life Skills and Trinity GESE Grade 5) focus on:

**Speaking:**
- A short conversation with an examiner or fellow candidate
- Giving and responding to information on everyday topics
- Expressing opinions and preferences

**Listening:**
- Understanding announcements, instructions and conversations
- Identifying key information in short audio clips

The tests are typically 20–30 minutes long. You do not need to write an essay — the focus is on communication.
